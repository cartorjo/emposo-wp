## How to read this

`facts.md` is a deliberately minimal, public-safe source, and it states its own
rule: *anything not listed must appear as a `«bestätigen»` placeholder.* The
live copy is the approved static site, which carries far more specificity than
the fact sheet authorises. So most findings below are not "the copy is wrong" —
they are **"the copy asserts something the public-safe fact sheet does not
cover; confirm it is approved, or mark it `«bestätigen»`."** Two findings are
different: they look like genuine contradictions worth checking first.

No copy was changed. Copy is DOM-parity-checked against the frozen reference, so
any wording change has to go through the exporter and a re-baseline, not a
template edit — these are decisions for the copy owner, filed as issues.
